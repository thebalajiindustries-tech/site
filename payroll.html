Salary Due = full salary for the month (you set it). Actual Salary = what was really paid (type it, or leave blank to use the Zoho figure). Adv. Deducted auto-fills as Due − Actual; edit it if the deduction differs — the rest shows as "unpaid". A payroll month runs from the "cycle starts on day" you set (e.g. 14 Mar – 13 Apr), not the calendar month. Advance Outstanding = advance taken (from Zoho) minus all deductions made in this tool across months. Nothing negative is written to Zoho Books — deductions are managed entirely inside this tool and saved on this computer.
