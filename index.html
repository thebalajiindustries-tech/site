About us

The Balaji Industries operates a fettling and finishing facility at Vambori in Ahmednagar district, Maharashtra. We take raw castings supplied by our customers and return them finished to specification — gates and risers removed, surfaces dressed, dimensions checked.

The work is carried out on a job-work basis: the castings remain the customer's property throughout, and we are paid per piece for the operations performed. That model keeps our customers' inventory on their own books while giving them a finishing line they don't have to run themselves.

We employ a workforce of over seventy on the shop floor, working to each customer's routing sheet, with every batch inspected before dispatch.

Services

A complete post-casting finishing line under one roof, so a casting leaves us ready for machining or assembly.

⚒

Fettling

Removal of gates, risers, runners and flash from raw castings, bringing the part down to its intended profile.

⚙

Grinding & deburring

Surface dressing, parting-line grinding and edge deburring to the tolerances set out on the customer drawing.

✨

Ultra-polish grinding (UPG)

Higher-grade finishing for components where surface quality matters — hydraulic passages, sealing faces and visible surfaces.

▿

Grit blasting

Abrasive blasting to clean sand, scale and oxide from castings and to prepare a uniform surface for coating.

♨

Acid pickling

Chemical cleaning to strip residual scale and burnt-on sand where blasting alone will not reach.

✅

Inspection & dispatch

Piece-wise checking against the routing sheet, with counts reconciled and batches packed for return to the customer.

How we work

Most of our customers send castings in scheduled batches against a rate contract, part number by part number. We work to their drawings and routing sheets rather than our own, which keeps the finished part consistent with everything else in their supply chain.

  • Per-part rate contracts, priced by operation
  • Batch scheduling to the customer's despatch plan
  • Work carried out on customer-owned material (job work)
  • Multiple operations combined in a single pass through our line
  • Piece counts reconciled and documented on return
  • GST-compliant invoicing under SAC 998860

Materials handled

Our line is set up for the cast components used in fluid-handling equipment — pump casings, impellers, covers, valve bodies and similar parts.

  • Grey cast iron
  • Ductile / SG iron
  • Cast steel
  • Stainless steel castings
  • Bronze and non-ferrous castings
